03 / About

Research and instruction in dialogue.

A↗

Dr. Ercan brings together e-learning architecture, serious game development, and research in Adult and Continuing Education at Friedrich Schiller University Jena.

With a PhD in Adult and Continuing Education and an MSc in Curriculum and Instruction, her practice spans Articulate Rise and Storyline, SCORM delivery, Moodle and Frontline, Unity-based game prototyping, UX/UI, and mixed-methods evaluation. She has designed and taught courses at METU and FSU Jena in English and German.

A PATTERN ACROSS THE PORTFOLIO

Guided inquiry, built into
the learning system.

Across these courses and prototypes, Dr. Ercan works as facilitator and learning architect, designing the conditions for inquiry: focused questions, sources worth examining, cases that make theory usable, and space for different interpretations.

In adult education, selected resources guide case-based tasks, discussion, and reflective writing. Students interpret evidence and develop reasoned responses. The course assistant helps them find approved material between sessions and return to the source; academic judgment, feedback, and the direction of learning remain with the instructor.

01 / FRAMEQuestions and sources create direction.
02 / INVESTIGATECases and discussion test interpretations.
03 / REFLECTLearners develop their own responses.
