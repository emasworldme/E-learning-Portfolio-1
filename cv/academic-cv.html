ACADEMIC PROJECTS

Doctoral Research Project: Development and Evaluation of a Digital Femalepreneurship Game Oct 2021 – Dec 2025

Friedrich-Schiller-Universität Jena, Germany - PhD dissertation project

Comprised four interrelated sub-projects/studies:

· Needs Analysis and Conceptualisation of Femalepreneurship Education: literature review, needs assessment and identification of entrepreneurial, risk-taking, resilience, and gender-related learning needs.

· Digital Game Design and Prototype Development: design and development of a serious game incorporating entrepreneurship education, risk management, resilience, and gender-related challenges, followed by iterative prototype/alpha testing.

· Scale Construction: usability and learning experience evaluation, refinement of the game and development/pilot testing of quantitative and qualitative research instruments.

· Cross-Cultural Pre–Post Evaluation: empirical evaluation of the final game with university students in Germany and Türkiye, examining changes in entrepreneurial competencies, risk-taking, resilience, awareness of gender-based discrimination, and the overall learning/game experience.

· The dissertation followed a monograph format rather than a cumulative/article-based dissertation model; therefore, the dissertation was conducted as a fully independent research project, with the research conception, methodological decisions, study implementation, data collection, analysis, and dissertation writing carried out by the doctoral researcher. The academic chair/supervisor provided doctoral supervision but was not involved as a co-researcher in the individual studies. Under the chair's publication policy, supervisors/advisors were therefore not listed as co-authors of thesis-related publications, and thesis-related articles could not be published during the dissertation-writing phase. Related publications were pursued only after dissertation submission/defense.
