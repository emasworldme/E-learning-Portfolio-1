01 / Learning approach

Work with a real problem. Ask better questions.

The course compares action learning and project-based learning in adult education. It introduces Reg Revans' approach, distinguishes open problems from puzzles, explores the Leuven PBL model, and ends with choices learners can apply to their own teaching context.

01 — INVESTIGATE

Action learning

Work from an unresolved situation, form questions before giving advice, and examine how action and reflection inform each other.

02 — DESIGN

Project-based learning

Move from a real project brief to collaborative decisions, while considering purpose, participation, and limits.

03 — TRANSFER

Practice decisions

Compare the approaches in adult education scenarios and identify a useful next step for one's own practice.
